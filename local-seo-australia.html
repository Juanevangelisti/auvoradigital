SEO & Local SEO

What is Local SEO and Why Your Business Needs It

June 2026 · 5 min read
JE
Juan Evangelisti
Digital Marketing Specialist · Auvora Digital

If you run a local business — a café, a tradesperson service, a boutique hotel or a retail shop — there's one question that matters more than almost anything else in digital marketing: when someone nearby searches for what you offer, does your business show up?

That's exactly what Local SEO is designed to solve. And in 2026, with more people than ever searching on their phones before making a purchase or booking, getting it right can make a significant difference to your revenue.

What is Local SEO?

Local SEO (Search Engine Optimisation) is the practice of optimising your online presence so your business appears in Google search results when people nearby are looking for your products or services.

Think about the last time you searched for something like "coffee near me", "plumber Sunshine Coast" or "best restaurant Noosa". The businesses that appeared at the top of those results — especially in the map pack — were there because of Local SEO.

Key insight: Nearly half of all Google searches have local intent — meaning people are looking for something in a specific area. If your business isn't optimised for local search, you're invisible to those potential customers.

Local SEO vs Regular SEO — What's the Difference?

Regular SEO focuses on ranking for broad keywords nationally or globally — think "best running shoes" or "digital marketing agency". Local SEO narrows the focus to a specific geographic area, targeting searches like "running shoes Noosa" or "digital marketing Sunshine Coast".

The goal is to appear in two key places on Google:

  • The Map Pack — the three business listings that appear with a map at the top of local search results
  • Organic search results — the traditional blue links below the map

Appearing in the Map Pack alone can dramatically increase the number of people who find and contact your business.

Why Does Local SEO Matter for Australian Businesses?

1. Mobile search is everywhere

Most local searches happen on mobile phones — often while people are out and about. When someone is in Noosa looking for a café, they're searching on their phone right now. Local SEO makes sure your business appears when it matters most.

2. High intent — people are ready to buy

Local searches have very high purchase intent. Someone searching "roadworthy Sunshine Coast" or "holiday apartments Noosa" isn't just browsing — they're ready to book or buy. Showing up for those searches puts you in front of motivated customers.

3. Less competition than national SEO

Competing nationally is incredibly difficult. But ranking for local searches in your specific area is far more achievable — especially if your competitors haven't optimised their local presence yet.

4. Google Business Profile is free

One of the most powerful tools in Local SEO — your Google Business Profile — is completely free. A well-optimised profile with accurate information, photos and regular updates can significantly boost your visibility in local search results.

The Key Elements of Local SEO

  • Google Business Profile — set up, verified and fully optimised with accurate details, photos and regular posts
  • Local keywords — using location-specific terms throughout your website content, titles and meta descriptions
  • NAP consistency — your Name, Address and Phone number must be identical across all online directories
  • Local citations — mentions of your business on directories like Yellow Pages, True Local and industry-specific sites
  • Google reviews — quantity and quality of reviews is a major ranking factor for local search
  • On-page SEO — location pages, service area content and locally relevant copy on your website

Is Local SEO Right for Your Business?

Local SEO works best for businesses that serve customers in a specific geographic area. If any of these sound like you, Local SEO should be a priority:

  • You have a physical location customers visit
  • You service customers in a specific area — tradies, cleaners, mobile services
  • You run a hospitality business — café, restaurant, hotel or holiday accommodation
  • You want more phone calls, bookings or foot traffic from nearby customers

Real example: A mobile roadworthy service on the Sunshine Coast with no Local SEO presence is invisible when someone searches "roadworthy near me" — even if they're the best in the area. The business that shows up first gets the booking.

How Long Does Local SEO Take?

Unlike paid ads which deliver instant visibility, Local SEO is a longer-term investment. Most businesses start seeing meaningful results within 60 to 90 days of implementing a proper Local SEO strategy. The key is consistency — regular updates to your Google Business Profile, accumulating reviews and building local citations over time.

The good news: once you rank well locally, that visibility compounds. You're not paying per click — every search that finds you is free organic traffic.
